# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in gopurs to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and precise when discussing technical disagreements.
- Assume good faith, but accept correction when evidence shows a mistake.
- Give credit for ideas, code, measurements and reviews.
- Keep criticism focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Threats, doxxing or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
chat and other project spaces, and when someone represents the project in
public.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. If a public
issue is inappropriate, use the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub
profile or the private advisory channel used by the project. Do not open a
public issue that identifies the person affected.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith. Maintainers will limit disclosure to
what is needed to investigate, avoid conflicts of interest and apply a
proportionate response. Possible responses include a private warning,
moderation, temporary restriction or removal from project spaces.
